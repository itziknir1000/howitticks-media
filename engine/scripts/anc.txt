How do headphones delete sound?
Sound is a wave. Air gets pushed, then pulled.
A tiny microphone on the outside hears the noise first.
A chip flips that wave upside down. Every push becomes a pull.
The speaker plays this anti-sound under your music.
Wave meets anti-wave, and they cancel out.
It works best on steady hums, like a jet engine.
Sudden sounds are harder, so the cushions block those.
So your headphones don't block the noise. They play its opposite.
